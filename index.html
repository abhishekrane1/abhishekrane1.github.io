About

I began in desktop support, moved into SQL reporting, and now design the data models, pipelines and dashboards that business teams rely on every day.

My tools are Snowflake, Power BI, QlikSense, Azure Data Factory and SQL. I also care about the unglamorous parts: data validation, consistent KPI definitions and documentation that lets the next person pick up my work.

I have worked with global stakeholders, including an on-site assignment in Italy.
